MR. A'S SCHEDULE
2:00 a.m.  Theatre 1.
2:14 a.m.  Records.
2:40 a.m.  Security.
3:00 a.m.  The roof.
Every appointment in the building, kept in order, one after another.
---
ROOF ACCESS
The stairwell is the only way out of this floor.
The door opens for anyone who carries the ledger, and for no one else.
It does not stay open for long. When the building wakes, the doors seal.
---
THE LEDGER
Every name in this book was a patient. Every patient was a guest.
Mr. A kept it so that no one would ever be forgotten.
Take it, and the building will not let you leave quietly.
---
VISITOR LOG
Visitors must sign in. The last signature is yours.
It is in your own handwriting, and it is dated tomorrow.
