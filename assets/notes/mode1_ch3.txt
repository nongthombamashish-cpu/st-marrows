ELEVATOR B: ARRIVAL NOTICE
Basement. Records. Staff only.
The car will not take you back up. Mr. A had the up button removed years ago.
The archive is to the east. The vault is on the far side of the stacks.
---
VAULT DOOR NOTICE
The vault opens when all three files are in the tray: Patient 9, Patient 12, Patient 17.
Mr. A's name is not in the register. His handwriting is on every page.
---
SHELF CARD: BOTTLES
Empty bottles from the pharmacy were left all over the archive. Nobody remembers why.
It follows sound. A bottle thrown down an aisle will pull it away from you. Two, if you need the time.
Do not throw one near yourself.
---
CLERK'S MEMO: MISSING FILES
Three files are missing from the index: Patient 9, Patient 12, Patient 17. Mr. A took them himself.
They were never returned. They are filed under the wrong letters on purpose: one in the office, one in the stacks, one by the incinerator.
