WARNING, SCRAWLED ON THE WALL
Your flashlight is a beacon. It sees the light from far away.
Turn it off and it has to come close to find you.
Lockers are safe, but only if it does not watch you climb in.
---
KEY LIST
Three keys lock the exit of Ward A: the pharmacy key, the records key and the night nurse's key.
Someone scattered them through the wards. The exit will not open without all three.
---
NIGHT NURSE'S LOG
It walks the corridors all night. It never runs, but it never stops either.
If it hears you running, it comes. Walk, and it only hears you up close.
---
TORN PAGE
St. Marrow's was built by Mr. A. The walls here were never meant to keep people out.
