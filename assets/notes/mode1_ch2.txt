WHITEBOARD: BATTERIES
Torch batteries are nearly gone. Whoever finds one, keep it for the dark parts.
It does not need to see you. It only needs a light to follow.
Off in the corridors, on in the rooms. Or the other way round. Nobody agrees which is worse.
---
ELEVATOR SERVICE TAG
Car B. One stop: down.
The panel has no floor numbers. Someone scratched a name into the plate: MR. A.
If the car ever arrives, do not wait for the doors to open twice.
---
THEATRE 1: OPERATION RECORD
Surgeon: Mr. A. Assistants: none.
The lights failed at 2:00. When they came back the table was empty and Mr. A was washing his hands.
He said the patient would walk again. He did not say where.
---
WING B: POWER NOTICE
The surgery wing runs on one fuse box, in the boiler room on the east side.
Three fuses were pulled and hidden: the sterilising room, the pharmacy, Theatre 1.
The elevator will not move without power. When the breaker catches, the whole wing will hear it.
